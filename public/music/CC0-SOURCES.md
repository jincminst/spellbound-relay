Every listed source page explicitly marks the track as CC0/public domain, so commercial use, redistribution, and modification are permitted and attribution is not required. The soundtrack uses one creator throughout to retain the piano, rock, electronic, orchestral, and chiptune blend established by `Bossfight_`.
